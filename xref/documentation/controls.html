Pan Canvas

Middle Click + Drag
Alt + Left Click + Drag
Shift + Ctrl + Mouse Wheel (pan vertically)
Shift + Mouse wheel (pan horizontally)

Zoom Canvas

Ctrl + Mouse Wheel
Ctrl + Middle Click + Drag

Zoom in on Item

Double-click on item
Double-click again to get back to previous view

Select Items

Single Item: Left Click
Multiple Items: Ctrl + Left Click
Rubberband Selection: Left Click + Drag

Move Selected Items

Left Click + Drag

Snap Rotation

Shift or Ctrl while rotating

Move Window

Ctrl + Alt + Left Click + Drag

Edit Text

Double Click on Text
Press Enter or click elsewhere to stop editing
Shift + Enter for newline

Full Screen

Double-click on the empty canvas
F11 toggles full screen
Escape leaves full screen

Window

XRef has no title bar. Move the window by dragging the 10 px wide area just inside any window edge (Settings -> Miscellaneous -> Window Edge Move Area), or with Ctrl + Alt + Left Click + Drag, or with Ctrl + M
Resize it by dragging the outermost pixels of any edge or corner
View -> Show Title Bar brings the normal window frame back
View -> Always On Top keeps the window above other applications (remembered between sessions)

Video, PDF and More

Hold the mouse over a video or PDF: scrub through the frames / flip through the pages (red playhead)
Single click: lock the frame or page under the pointer (padlock); single click again to browse
Double click on a video: play / pause
Double-click on a PDF: next page
Arrow buttons in the control bar: previous / next page

Pen and Links

Double-tap Alt to toggle pen mode (Escape to leave it)
Drag from a media item's link port to connect two items
Click a link and press Delete to remove it
Ctrl + Drag moves linked items together
