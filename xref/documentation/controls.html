Navigate 3D Models

Dragging a 3D model with the left mouse button moves it on the canvas like any other item. To look at the model from different angles, hold Ctrl (or Cmd) and click it: the model's own camera takes over and follows three.js OrbitControls exactly. Left-drag then orbits, right-drag pans, middle-drag or the mouse wheel zooms, and Shift swaps orbit and pan (0.05 damping factor, 0.95 zoom steps). The camera keeps its up axis, so the model never rolls over, and nothing is drawn on top of the model. Press Ctrl and click the model again, or press Escape, to go back to moving it

Before / After Comparison

Select two images and hold Alt while moving the pointer over either one to reveal the image below it; release Alt to restore the full image
Choose Arrange -> Match Before/After or press Ctrl + Shift + M to align the images persistently

Window

Window Buttons

Rest the pointer in the top right corner for a moment: minimize, full screen and close buttons appear
The delay is configurable (Settings -> Miscellaneous -> Window Buttons Delay)

XRef has no title bar. Right-click and drag anywhere to move the window. You can also use Ctrl + Alt + Left Click + Drag, or Ctrl + Alt + M
Ctrl + M minimizes the window; Ctrl + F toggles maximized / normal size
Resize it by dragging the outermost pixels of any edge or corner
View -> Show Title Bar brings the normal window frame back
View -> Always On Top keeps the window above other applications (remembered between sessions)

Video, PDF and More

Hold the mouse over a video or PDF: scrub through the frames / flip through the pages (red playhead)
Single click: lock the frame or page under the pointer (padlock); single click again to browse
Double click on a video: play / pause
Double-click on a PDF: next page
Arrow buttons in the control bar: previous / next page

Pen and Links

Double-tap Alt to toggle pen mode (Escape to leave it)
Drag from a media item's link port to connect two items
Click a link and press Delete to remove it
Ctrl + Drag moves linked items together
